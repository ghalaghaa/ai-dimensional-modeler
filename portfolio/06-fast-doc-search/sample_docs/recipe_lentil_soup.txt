Simple Red Lentil Soup

Ingredients:
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- Juice of half a lemon
- Salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Saute the onion and carrots in olive oil until soft, around 6 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, and turmeric. Cook for one more minute.
3. Add the rinsed lentils and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then
   reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils are tender.
4. Blend partially for a creamier texture, or leave chunky if preferred.
5. Stir in lemon juice and season with salt before serving.

This lentil soup recipe is cheap, vegetarian, freezer-friendly, and ready
in under 40 minutes. Great for a quick weeknight meal or meal prep lunch.
