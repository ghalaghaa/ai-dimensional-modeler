Chicken Curry Recipe

Ingredients:
- 500g chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until
   softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in garlic and ginger, cook for another minute until fragrant.
3. Add curry powder and toast briefly, then add the chicken and brown on
   all sides.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 25 minutes
   until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
6. Serve the curry hot over steamed rice.

This chicken curry is a family favorite for weeknight dinners and also
freezes well for meal prep.
