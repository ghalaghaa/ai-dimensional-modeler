Gardening Notes: Growing Tomatoes

Planting:
- Start seeds indoors 6-8 weeks before the last frost date.
- Harden off seedlings for about a week before transplanting outside.
- Space tomato plants 18-24 inches apart in full sun, at least 6 hours
  of direct sunlight per day.
- Use a support cage or stake early to avoid damaging roots later.

Watering and feeding:
- Water deeply and consistently, roughly 1-2 inches per week. Inconsistent
  watering causes blossom end rot and cracked fruit.
- Feed with a balanced fertilizer at planting, then switch to a
  low-nitrogen, higher-potassium feed once flowering starts.
- Mulch around the base to retain moisture and suppress weeds.

Common problems:
- Yellowing lower leaves: often a nitrogen deficiency or normal aging,
  remove affected leaves.
- Blossom drop: caused by temperature extremes, usually resolves on its
  own once conditions moderate.
- Hornworms: large green caterpillars that strip leaves quickly, hand
  pick them off in the early morning.

Harvest: pick tomatoes when they are fully colored but still slightly
firm, and let them finish ripening indoors if frost threatens.

This year I am growing Cherokee Purple, Sun Gold cherry tomatoes, and a
few Roma plants for sauce.
