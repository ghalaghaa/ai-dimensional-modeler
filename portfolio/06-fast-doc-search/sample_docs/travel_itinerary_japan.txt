Travel Itinerary: Japan Trip
Dates: October 3 - October 14

Day 1-2: Tokyo
- Arrive at Narita Airport, take the express train to Shinjuku.
- Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa and explore the street food stalls.
- Evening: dinner in Shibuya, walk across the famous scramble crossing.

Day 3-4: Tokyo (continued)
- Day trip to Nikko to see the shrines and waterfalls.
- Explore Akihabara for electronics and anime shops.
- Try an omakase sushi dinner near Tsukiji outer market.

Day 5: Travel to Kyoto
- Take the Shinkansen bullet train from Tokyo to Kyoto, about 2.5 hours.
- Check into hotel near Gion district.
- Evening walk through Gion to spot geisha and traditional teahouses.

Day 6-7: Kyoto
- Fushimi Inari shrine at sunrise to avoid crowds under the torii gates.
- Kinkaku-ji Golden Pavilion and Arashiyama bamboo grove.
- Tea ceremony experience booked for day 7 afternoon.

Day 8-9: Osaka
- Train to Osaka. Visit Osaka Castle and Dotonbori for street food.
- Day trip option to Nara to see the deer park and Todai-ji temple.

Day 10-11: Hiroshima
- Visit the Peace Memorial Park and Museum.
- Ferry to Miyajima Island to see the floating torii gate.

Day 12: Return to Tokyo, free day for shopping in Ginza.
Day 13-14: Flight home from Haneda Airport.

Packing reminders: comfortable walking shoes, portable wifi router,
JR Rail Pass, adapter for Japanese outlets, cash for smaller vendors.
