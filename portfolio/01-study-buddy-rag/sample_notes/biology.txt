Cell Biology Basics

The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell, producing ATP through
cellular respiration. It has its own DNA, separate from the nucleus,
which supports the theory that mitochondria were once independent
organisms absorbed by early eukaryotic cells.

Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in
glucose. It takes place in the chloroplasts of plant cells and involves
two main stages: the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle.
Oxygen is released as a byproduct of the light-dependent reactions.

Cell Division

Mitosis is the process by which a single cell divides into two
genetically identical daughter cells. It consists of prophase, metaphase,
anaphase, and telophase. Meiosis, in contrast, produces four genetically
distinct gametes and is essential for sexual reproduction.
